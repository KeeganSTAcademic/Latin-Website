Passive verbs

Passive verbs are those in which the action is done to the subject rather than the subject doing the action. In the Latin incomplete system (present, future, and imperfect) these verbs are formed by adding a second set of personal endings to the same stem, linking vowel, and time marker as the active.

Note that these personal endings look, with the exception of -minī for the 2nd plural, mostly like the active, just with an r somewhere in them.

We translate them in the present like "he is loved" or "he is being loved," in the imperfect like "he was being loved," and in the future like "he will be loved" or "he will be being loved."

The future passive is shown for the 1st and 2nd conjugations only, like the future active page.
