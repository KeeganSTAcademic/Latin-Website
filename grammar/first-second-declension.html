First & Second Declension

An interactive grammar for puella, equus, and forum

The two most regular declensions, and the place to start. The first is built on a, the second on o (usually written u), and many adjectives use both: bonus, bona, bonum.

More 2nd declension: nouns in -er

A few masculine nouns end in -er instead of -us. They have no ending in the nominative singular, and after that they're identical to equus. The genitive tells you whether the e stays (puer, puerī) or drops out (ager, agrī).
