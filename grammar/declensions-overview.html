How to read this page

Each column is one declension, using the model nouns from the individual pages: puella, equus, forum, rēx, corpus, frūctus, cornū, rēs. Neuters get their own columns because their endings differ from masculine and feminine nouns.

Each row is one case and number. Read across a row to see how one job is marked in every declension, and down a column to see a single declension's endings.

The big letters are the endings; the small word underneath is the full form. Hover an ending to see its translation, what else it could be, and which patterns it belongs to. Use the pattern buttons to light up the rules that hold across declensions, including the cross-declension look-alikes that trip readers up most often.
