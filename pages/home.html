Latin at GHH

Salvēte discipulī et discipulae! Magistrī Tostevin, Duplantis, and Russell warmly welcome all students to our website for Upperschool Latin at Great Hearts Harveston. We have produced this site to serve as a repository of references, resources, and games to aid in your journey in the language. We recognize that many are remediating and reviewing concepts due to disruptions in instruction from prior years and that we cannot be everywhere at once. We hope that these materials can serve as an extra repository to assist your learning. To find class specific information, check the class listings on the top left.

Elements will be added gradually over time, but the site currently offers flash cards, some rudimentary practice games, an interactive grammar for nouns, and links to Quizlet, Anki, and other useful resources.

Update 10/3/25: The flashcard system is now functional and even more advanced than Quizlet. Quizlet will continue to be maintained but the site is the superior option for digital flash cards.
