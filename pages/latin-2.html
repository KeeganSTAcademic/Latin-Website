Latin II

Course Overview: Latin II continues students' formal instruction in the grammar of Latin and covers all indicative forms of the verb (including the imperfect, future, perfect, pluperfect, and future perfect tenses), the full pronoun system, and participles. It aims to prepare students for completing their grammatical sequence in Latin III where they will encounter the subjunctive, the clauses it governs, and 'points of art' in translation.
