The DCC Core Vocabulary

The DCC Core is a list of the 1,000 most common words in the Latin language. Students should be working to actively master it in order to facilitate smooth translation without over reliance on a dictionary. You can find it listed in groups of 50 in the Quizlet and on the handout given during the first week of class.

Open the DCC Core list

The Perseus Library

Maintained by Tufts University, the Perseus Project is an open source collection of ancient texts attached to a word parsing tool. Almost a generation of Latinists and Hellenists have now used it with opinions ranging from it being the death of grammatical learning to the most useful free tool ever made for classicists. In the age of AI these debates seem quaint and I provide it here as a tool to be consulted though not relied upon when preparing class translations.
