Latin III

Course Overview: Latin III finishes students' formal instruction in the grammar of Latin and covers the subjunctive, its various clauses (including clauses of purpose, result, fearing, deliberation, and conditionals), gerunds, gerundives, the supine, and the ablative absolute. At the end of Latin III students will be prepared to 'encounter the Romans' by studying real Latin authors in Latin IV.
