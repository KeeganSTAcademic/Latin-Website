Latin IV

Course Overview: Latin IV exposes students to real Latin authors through unadapted readings of the works of Eutropius, Catullus, and Horace among others. Students work to build reading fluency and familiarity with the complexities and (often) difficulties of interpreting real ancient texts. It is also at this level that students begin to use ancient texts as evidence to think more critically about the ancient world, its figures, and ideas in a manner comparable to a humane letters seminar.
